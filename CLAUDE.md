# INTUITY · Cambridge B2

A static site for teenage Cambridge B2 learners. Netlify publishes the `main` branch automatically.

## Going live

The owner wants finished changes live without asking:

- Commit straight to `main` and push. Do not open a pull request or leave work on a side branch, because Netlify only publishes `main`. Work left on branches never reaches the pupils (this happened on 9 Nov 2026 with four unmerged pull requests).
- Before pushing, check the changed pages: they open on a phone-sized screen (390 px wide) with no console errors, and any changed JSON is valid.
- If another session's work is waiting on a branch or in an open pull request, merge it into `main` once it passes the same checks.

## Working style

- Change things only when they help pupils learn. Skip anything pedagogically counterproductive.
- Every modal card shares one look: the FiveCard architecture (`tools/five-card.js`/`.css`).
- Speaking is practice now (owner's decision after the pupils' verdict, 23 Nov 2026: "too many entry gates"). The menu has four rings, one per Part, each opening `skills/speaking/test.html?part=N`: that Part only, practised at once (Maya, the examiner, asks; the pupil answers); that Part's topics in the header (never the other Parts); that Part's Structures, Drills and Games as footer pills (Writing's pill row). The whole test is the menu's Full test pill. Don't put a gate, step or explanation back in front of the practice. Structures, Drills and Games teach what Practise teaches (the same moves, labels, topics and chunks, from the Tomás and Beatriz dialogue files), and none of them lets a pupil switch to another Part. Writing follows the same way (owner's decision, 25 Nov 2026): a task's ring opens its Model at once (the entry: a page that needs no thinking, with a Watch ring for the task's clip; the gate is gone); the footer is Practise, Basics, Exercises, Game (`tools/writing-foot.js`), the last three options only; Practise is a slim track above the footer, Model, Question, Plan, Sample, Write it; back inside a task leads to its Model, the Model leads to the Writing menu, and nothing switches to another task.
- Use of English follows the same way (owner's decision, 25 Nov 2026): four rings, one per Part, each opening that Part as in the paper (its exam text) at once; the footer is Practise, Learn, Drill, Game (`tools/english-foot.js`): Learn is the Master page's Explore, Drill its Practice (one sentence at a time), Game the Part's Challenge board. Back from Learn, Drill and Game leads to the Part; the exam text leads to the menu; nothing switches to another Part. No counter or row of empty dots shows before the pupil has answered something. Use of English is criticism-free as Writing is (the boards' `CALM` flag covers its topics).
- Use of English and Reading menus share one shape (owner, 26 Nov 2026, as the Writing menu: hairlines between sections): The whole paper (Full tests, all seven Parts, 75 minutes); Half a paper (Full Use of English, Parts 1–4, 30 minutes; Full Reading, Parts 5–7, 45 minutes; side by side); then that menu's Parts. One page runs all three (`skills/full-test/full-test.html`, `?paper=english|reading`, `&from=` the menu to return to); each half keeps its own attempts and bests. No instruction page in front (owner, 26 Nov 2026): the paper opens on its first Part; a START | INSTRUCTIONS pill under the tabs gives the instructions one tap away; Start (or a first answer) starts the clock, and Start becomes it, counting up from 00:00 (handed in at the limit). Before the clock runs the tabs are the tests; once it runs, the Parts. Cambridge counts Part 1 towards Reading, so the Use of English half scores Parts 2–4 exactly and shows Part 1 in marks; the Reading half's score is an estimate and says so.
- Reading keeps the exam's two pages (owner's decision, 25 Nov 2026): text and questions are two pages a toggle apart (Parts 5 and 6 open on the Text, owner's decision 26 Nov 2026; Part 5 Text | Questions; Part 6 Text | Sentences, where tapping a gap opens the Sentences with that gap pinned and placing a sentence returns to the text at that gap; Part 7 Statements | Sections), never one long scroll and never a question-by-question view. Above each text stands the paper's rubric ("You are going to read an article about … For questions 1–6, choose…"; each text's `rubric` phrase in its JSON; Part 7's from its subtitle), and below it the text's title with the two pages beside it as a Play | List-style pill (e.g. TEXT | QUESTIONS), on both pages (owner, 26 Nov 2026: "next to the title of the text, more like PLAY & LIST"). Options and sentences read like the printed paper: plain rows, a small lettered ring, hairlines; never grey slabs ("the questions look very tense"). The pages switch from the pill beside the title and from the same toggle in the footer, which shares one row with Submit (owner 26 Nov 2026: "merge two footers into one"; Submit reads "6 to go" until every question is answered); each page keeps its own place when the pupil flips; the strip above the bar shows only when something is in hand; what is in hand stays pinned on the other page (Part 5 the question on the Text page; Part 6 the gap and the words before it on the Sentences page; Part 7 the statement and its letters on the Sections page). After Submit every answer, right ones too, shows its evidence in the text. No Show answer before a first try; no row to switch Parts; no counters or empty dots before an answer; criticism-free as Writing (data/reading/reading.js). Teach the coursebook method: Part 5 question, then the place in the text, then the paraphrase; Part 6 gist, then before/sentence/after; Part 7 text by text.
- Three rules for choosing (owner, 27 Nov 2026), set once in `tools/be2-brand.js` (and locally on the pages that don't load it: Speaking's test page, Writing's roof script, the Ladder): (1) which one, the header rows of sets, tests, texts, topics and sections, is words with the chosen one on the flat light grey iOS pill, rgba(118,118,128,.12), never an underline; (2) which view, a toggle (All | One at a time, Text | Questions, Start | Instructions, Cards | Phrases), is the Play | List pill: a light grey track, the chosen side black, small capitals; (3) which page, the footers (Practise · Learn · Drill · Game), stays the iOS white-on-grey. No beige and no caramel underline on any selector. Unselected words are one tone everywhere: #4A4541 (owner, 26 Nov 2026: "slightly darker, not too much").
- The two-row header of mode selectors and the pendulum gate are deliberate: once a pupil learns how one page works, every other page follows suit. Keep them; never count them as clutter in an audit.
- Judge a page first by whether it delivers what it promises pedagogically: the B2 First papers the app covers (Reading, Use of English, Writing, Speaking), and general grammar and vocabulary learning for pupils who use the app beyond the exam.
- Listening is not part of the app (owner's decision, 12 Nov 2026). Don't propose it, and don't claim the app covers all five skills or every paper.
- Pupils sit both B2 First and B2 First for Schools, prepared the same way (they are told the differences in writing, speaking and reading). Keep both the report and the story.
- Minimise taps. Games should have tension: a check, a score, a run and a personal best (`tools/intuity-cheer.js` for sound and glitter).
- Writing is criticism-free (owner's decision, 25 Nov 2026; the other menus follow one at a time). On a Writing page nothing the pupil does is called wrong, a mistake, missed, weak or a slip: a miss is "Not yet" with the answer shown, and it comes back next time. Nothing is taken away: no lives, no strikes that end a board, no step drops (a miss keeps its step), no D grade. No red: soft rust or caramel at most, and no crosses on tiles. Feedback on a pupil's own text leads with what they did well and gives at most three polishes. How it's marked shows what earns a 5 and a 3, never Band 1 as a verdict. A Portuguese-speaker slip is taught as a contrast between the two languages ("From Portuguese"), not as a warning. Tension stays: the check, the score (more for a first try), the clock, the run, the personal best. The shared boards (`skills/challenge/challenge.html`, `boardgame.html`, `tools/report-it.js`) do this only for `section=writing` (their `CALM` flag), so Grammar and Vocabulary are unchanged until their turn (Use of English and Reading joined on 25 Nov 2026).
- Bump the `?v=` query on a shared script or stylesheet when you change it, so phones don't keep an old copy.
