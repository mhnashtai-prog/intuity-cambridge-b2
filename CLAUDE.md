# INTUITY · Cambridge B2

A static site for teenage Cambridge B2 learners. Netlify publishes the `main` branch automatically.

## Going live

The owner wants finished changes live without asking:

- Commit straight to `main` and push. Do not open a pull request or leave work on a side branch, because Netlify only publishes `main`. Work left on branches never reaches the pupils (this happened on 9 Nov 2026 with four unmerged pull requests).
- Before pushing, check the changed pages: they open on a phone-sized screen (390 px wide) with no console errors, and any changed JSON is valid.
- If another session's work is waiting on a branch or in an open pull request, merge it into `main` once it passes the same checks.

## Working style

- Change things only when they help pupils learn. Skip anything pedagogically counterproductive.
- Every modal card shares one look: the FiveCard architecture (`tools/five-card.js`/`.css`).
- Speaking is practice now (owner's decision after the pupils' verdict, 23 Nov 2026: "too many entry gates"). The menu has four rings, one per Part, each opening `skills/speaking/test.html?part=N`: that Part only, practised at once (Maya, the examiner, asks; the pupil answers); that Part's topics in the header (never the other Parts); that Part's footer pills Practise · Learn · Drill · Game (Learn opens its Structures; Writing's pill row). The whole test is the menu's Full test pill. Don't put a gate, step or explanation back in front of the practice. Structures, Drills and Games teach what Practise teaches (the same moves, labels, topics and chunks, from the Tomás and Beatriz dialogue files), and none of them lets a pupil switch to another Part. Writing follows the same way (owner's decision, 25 Nov 2026): a task's ring opens its Model at once (the entry: a page that needs no thinking, with a Watch ring for the task's clip; the gate is gone); the footer is Practise, Learn, Drill, Game (`tools/writing-foot.js`; Learn opens The five, Drill opens Exercises), the last three options only; Practise is a slim track above the footer, Model, Question, Plan, Full answer, Write it; back inside a task leads to its Model, the Model leads to the Writing menu, and nothing switches to another task.
- Use of English follows the same way (owner's decision, 25 Nov 2026): four rings, one per Part, each opening that Part as in the paper (its exam text) at once; the footer is Practise, Learn, Drill, Game (`tools/english-foot.js`): Learn is the Master page's Explore, Drill its Practice (one sentence at a time), Game the Part's Challenge board. Back from Learn, Drill and Game leads to the Part; the exam text leads to the menu; nothing switches to another Part. No counter or row of empty dots shows before the pupil has answered something. A Part's numbered drills are Sets (Set 1…), never Tests: a test is a whole paper (owner, 27 Nov 2026). Use of English is criticism-free as Writing is (the boards' `CALM` flag covers its topics).
- Use of English and Reading menus share one shape (owner, 26 Nov 2026, as the Writing menu: hairlines between sections): The whole paper (Full tests, all seven Parts, 75 minutes); Half a paper (Full Use of English, Parts 1–4, 30 minutes; Full Reading, Parts 5–7, 45 minutes; side by side); then that menu's Parts. One page runs all three (`skills/full-test/full-test.html`, `?paper=english|reading`, `&from=` the menu to return to); each half keeps its own attempts and bests. No instruction page in front (owner, 26 Nov 2026): the paper opens on its first Part; a START | INSTRUCTIONS pill under the tabs gives the instructions one tap away; Start (or a first answer) starts the clock, and Start becomes it, counting up from 00:00 (handed in at the limit). Before the clock runs the tabs are the tests; once it runs, the Parts. A test is no distraction tool (owner, 27 Nov 2026): during the paper nothing leads away from it, no options footer, one clock (the header's), only ‹ Part · Part › · answered and the flags, with Finish on the last Part alone (owner, 27 Nov 2026, after 25 pupils asked whether to submit after each Part: one hand-in, at the end; checking a Part on its own belongs to the Part's own page); a Part's tab shows its count only once it has an answer. The options come after Finish: the results name the Part with most marks to win back and give its own footer (Use of English: Practise · Learn · Drill · Game; Reading: Practise). Cambridge counts Part 1 towards Reading, so the Use of English half scores Parts 2–4 exactly and shows Part 1 in marks; the Reading half's score is an estimate and says so.
- Reading keeps the exam's two pages (owner's decision, 25 Nov 2026): text and questions are two pages a toggle apart (Parts 5 and 6 open on the Text, owner's decision 26 Nov 2026; Part 5 Text | Questions; Part 6 Text | Sentences, where tapping a gap opens the Sentences with that gap pinned and placing a sentence returns to the text at that gap; Part 7 Statements | Sections), never one long scroll and never a question-by-question view. Above each text stands the paper's rubric ("You are going to read an article about … For questions 1–6, choose…"; each text's `rubric` phrase in its JSON; Part 7's from its subtitle), and below it the text's title with the two pages beside it as a Play | List-style pill (e.g. TEXT | QUESTIONS), on both pages (owner, 26 Nov 2026: "next to the title of the text, more like PLAY & LIST"). Options and sentences read like the printed paper: plain rows, a small lettered ring, hairlines; never grey slabs ("the questions look very tense"). The pages switch from the pill beside the title and from the same toggle in the footer, which shares one row with Submit (owner 26 Nov 2026: "merge two footers into one"; Submit reads "6 to go" until every question is answered); each page keeps its own place when the pupil flips; the strip above the bar shows only when something is in hand; what is in hand stays pinned on the other page (Part 5 the question on the Text page; Part 6 the gap and the words before it on the Sentences page; Part 7 the statement and its letters on the Sections page). After Submit every answer, right ones too, shows its evidence in the text. No Show answer before a first try; no row to switch Parts; no counters or empty dots before an answer; criticism-free as Writing (data/reading/reading.js). Teach the coursebook method: Part 5 question, then the place in the text, then the paraphrase; Part 6 gist, then before/sentence/after; Part 7 text by text.
- Grammar keeps its own shape (owner, 27 Nov 2026): the header's Learn · Practise · Play stays (no Practise · Learn · Drill · Game footer), and pupils always pick a set first (Gap-fill's and Defuse's "Pick a set above" stay). Nothing picks a set for them (28 Nov 2026: `tools/topic-start.js` no longer starts the last set, and the gate no longer steps aside on a slow phone); ← Grammar always leads to the Grammar menu (`/index.html?openModal=grammar`), never `history.back()`, which an embedded frame's history could swallow. Don't propose changing either. The Rules blueprint (owner, 28 Nov 2026, from Vince's First Certificate Language Practice: rules to consult if necessary, then varied exercises), on Conditionals first and then every grammar topic (owner, 28 Nov 2026: "all pages … can easily inherit this new look"; the cards and Rules everywhere, the `lines` and Why? where a topic's rules have them, Conditionals so far): the header reads Rules · Practise · Play (`data-learn="Rules"` on the mode row), and under Rules no second row: a group with one mode hides its row (owner, 28 Nov 2026: no Explore pill under Rules, the card has its own); Explore stays the way in, full screen, and opens straight on the set's first card, no map (owner, 28 Nov 2026: "the menu is removed but these cards can stay and be navigated"; `&deck=1` to explore-map.html): ‹ › and a swipe walk the cards and on into the next set; each card's title is the form itself, then the example and its other order; the rule's `lines` (Use, Tip, Watch out, From Portuguese, in short dark rows) stand on each set's first card only, so the rest stay light: the explanation lives on the card (owner, 28 Nov 2026: a rule page that repeated the list's sentences was tried and reverted); after a Not yet, Gap-fill shows the form and a Why? (Defuse: in its summary, never during the clock) that opens the same rule as a FiveCard (`tools/rule-card.js`). A topic joins by writing `lines` on its rules in its data file and the attribute on its row. Past Modals' Play has a second game, Odd one out (`past-modal-game.html`, 27 Nov 2026): it tests what a past modal means, not its form; criticism-free (no stars lost, no grade, more for a first try, a personal best).
- Writing's words, one meaning each (owner, 27 Nov 2026, after pupils got lost): the Model's toggle is Outline | Phrases; Learn's tabs are The five · Build your own · Ready-made; the track's fourth step is Full answer (the Model is the outline); the menu items are Phrase ladder and Revision. Don't reuse Cards, Basics, Sample or Test yourself as labels. Write it has a Type | On paper toggle (27 Nov 2026, from the old Desk mode): On paper shows the question as the exam sets it out and a clock counting up (about 40 minutes), then Check it and Type it up. The Story Model's Phrases show each bank in three levels (Plain, B2, Ambitious).
- One set of footer words on every menu (owner, 27 Nov 2026): Practise · Learn · Drill · Game, the same four slots everywhere (do the exam task; understand the language; repeat it; use it under pressure). The page inside a slot keeps its own name in its header (The five, Structures, Exercises).
- Three rules for choosing (owner, 27 Nov 2026), set once in `tools/be2-brand.js` (and locally on the pages that don't load it: Speaking's test page, Writing's roof script, the Ladder): (1) which one, the header rows of sets, tests, texts, topics and sections, is words with the chosen one on the flat light grey iOS pill, rgba(118,118,128,.12), never an underline; (2) which view, a toggle (All | One at a time, Text | Questions, Start | Instructions, Cards | Phrases), is the Play | List pill: a light grey track, the chosen side white (flat, owner 28 Nov 2026: "all the pages across the app"; it was black until then), small capitals; (3) which page, the footers (Practise · Learn · Drill · Game), stays the iOS white-on-grey. No beige and no caramel underline on any selector. A page with its own row classes joins the lists in be2-brand.js (`TOG` for toggles, `ROW` for which-one rows); a row of pages inside a `.foot` is rule 3, never a black toggle. Checked by opening every reachable page (27 Nov 2026: 125 pages, none left beyond Listen | Speak, which are actions, and progress dots). The header is flat (owner, 28 Nov 2026: "more flat and less intense", after the Claude app's own pills): the Learn/Rules · Practise · Play switch is one light grey track with the chosen side white, no outer ring; the chosen mode pill under it is a lower, lighter grey (34px tall, its 44px touch area kept invisibly). Unselected words are one tone everywhere: #4A4541 (owner, 26 Nov 2026: "slightly darker, not too much").
- Help is one pill (owner, 27 Nov 2026): `tools/help-pill.js`, loaded by be2-brand.js (and directly on Speaking's test page and Writing's Exercises), bottom right just above the page's bars, opening a FiveCard (For · How · Tip) on tap and never by itself. It steps aside while a results card or other dialog is open (a results overlay carries role="dialog"). It shows only on pages with an entry in its HELP list, never on the full tests; a page's own Help button gives way to it. New help is one entry: what the page is for, three ways to use it, one tip, criticism-free.
- Search is one pill on the home page (owner, 27 Nov 2026), left of Daily 5 and in its style, showing only a magnifying glass (owner, 28 Nov 2026: "it is enough"): it searches the menus' own list (be2-map.js) plus a few words pupils type (its ALSO list in index.html), every word must match (plurals too: cards finds card), results name their menu, the page named so comes first, Enter opens the first. A Writing task's inside pages (The five, Build your own, Ready-made, Exercises, Recall, Game) are searchable too, built from be2-map's task roofs, and so are the pages one step inside (owner, 27 Nov 2026: "get the search to work fully"): each Grammar topic's Gap-fill, Rewrite, Challenge 1 and 2 and Defuse; Vocabulary's boards, Defuse and modes; each Use of English Part's Learn, Drill and Game; each Speaking Part's Structures, Drill and Game; Writing's roof tabs, How it's marked and How to use, each named with where it lives ("Tenses · Defuse"). A new page reached from inside a page joins that list in index.html. Nowhere else: on other pages it would be one more way out.
- The home page's six banners run in the exam's order, the papers first (owner, 27 Nov 2026, a pupil's idea): Use of English, Reading, Writing, Speaking, then Grammar, and Vocabulary last as many coursebooks put their word lists at the end (the banner colours keep their old order from top to bottom: caramel, sage, sand, tan, charcoal, caramel; they belong to the places, not the areas). A banner's number counts its activities, never its section headings. The landing page is minimalist (owner, 28 Nov 2026): labels kept to a minimum, so a banner's caption is only its short line of contents, never its name a second time.
- The pendulum is the moment of decision (owner, 28 Nov 2026: "the pendulum is the moment they have to make a decision; the start dial takes the viewer by surprise"; "a decluttered screen is always a gentle welcome with the pendulum to prepare the tense mind"): it stands only where the pupil chooses before anything happens (a set, a mix, a verb, a question and how to write it, a game's clock). A page with nothing to decide opens on its content, never behind a START dial (Speaking's Structures and Writing's The five open on their card, with Learn them; Hear the phrases on its list).
- The two-row header of mode selectors and the pendulum gate are deliberate: once a pupil learns how one page works, every other page follows suit. Keep them; never count them as clutter in an audit.
- Judge a page first by whether it delivers what it promises pedagogically: the B2 First papers the app covers (Reading, Use of English, Writing, Speaking), and general grammar and vocabulary learning for pupils who use the app beyond the exam.
- Listening is not part of the app (owner's decision, 12 Nov 2026). Don't propose it, and don't claim the app covers all five skills or every paper.
- Pupils sit both B2 First and B2 First for Schools, prepared the same way (they are told the differences in writing, speaking and reading). Keep both the report and the story.
- No warm-ups (owner, 28 Nov 2026: "one more tapping and reading that prevents immediate focus"): a set opens on its first item. Use of English Part 4's Exam level | Warm-up is a choice of level, not a step in front, and stays.
- A miss ties to Daily 5 (28 Nov 2026, a pupil's-eye audit): the first "Not yet" on a page that makes a review card says once, at the top, "This one comes back in your Daily 5" (`tools/be2-progress.js`); never on the full tests. In Use of English the top row of Parts 3 and 4 holds Packs, so a Set is never inside a Set.
- Minimise taps. Games should have tension: a check, a score, a run and a personal best (`tools/intuity-cheer.js` for sound and glitter).
- The whole app is criticism-free (it began with Writing, owner's decision 25 Nov 2026; Use of English and Reading joined on 25 Nov, Grammar and Vocabulary on 27 Nov). Nothing the pupil does is called wrong, a mistake, missed, weak or a slip: a miss is "Not yet" with the answer shown, and it comes back next time. Nothing is taken away: no lives, no health bars, no strikes that end a board, no step drops (a miss keeps its step), no D grade. No red: soft rust or caramel at most, and no crosses on tiles. No counter or row of empty dots before the first answer; game scores (Score, Right, the run) stay, for tension. Feedback on a pupil's own text leads with what they did well and gives at most three polishes. How it's marked shows what earns a 5 and a 3, never Band 1 as a verdict. A Portuguese-speaker slip is taught as a contrast between the two languages ("From Portuguese"), not as a warning. Tension stays: the check, the score (more for a first try), the clock, the run, the personal best. Where it lives: the shared boards (`skills/challenge/challenge.html`, `boardgame.html`) and `tools/report-it.js` have `CALM` always on; `tools/be2-brand.js` gives every page the calm miss colours; page games carry their own (Defuse: no health bar, no defeat, the round ends when the set is cleared or the clock runs out). Example sentences that contain such words ("a blindingly obvious mistake") are content, not criticism.
- Old pages are archived, not deleted (owner, 27 Nov 2026: "it can boost our creativity with old ideas"): `archive/` keeps each at its old address under `archive/`, with `archive/README.md` saying what each idea was and what replaced it. Nothing links there; look there before building something new. Older drafts are in `bin/`.
- Bump the `?v=` query on a shared script or stylesheet when you change it, so phones don't keep an old copy.
