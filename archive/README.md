# The archive

Old pages kept for their ideas (owner, 27 Nov 2026: "In case we need something, it can boost our creativity with old ideas").
No menu, search or link leads here, and pupils never see these pages. Each page sits at its old address under `archive/`, so `archive/skills/writing/essays/essay-slides.html` was `skills/writing/essays/essay-slides.html`.

Moving a page down one folder breaks its relative links (`../../data/…`), so some no longer load their data. To see one working, open its old address in an earlier commit (`git log --all -- skills/<path>`), or move it back.

Before building something new, look here first. To bring a page back: move it to its old place, make it follow CLAUDE.md (criticism-free, the three rules for choosing, the footer words), and give it a home in a menu, a Play row or a track.

Older drafts from before November live in `bin/`.
