BUYING TIME — Examiner + Samara (ElevenLabs Text to Dialogue)
Speaker 1 = EXAMINER (a calm, neutral voice of your choice)
Speaker 2 = SAMARA (Samara X)
Set the pace slower than the last take: about 0.9.
Three short batches, so the pace stays steady. Paste each batch as a dialogue,
one line per turn. Samara's intro, bare phrases and closing line are kept from
this morning's take: they were fine.

━━━━━━━━━━ BATCH 1 · Well / To be honest ━━━━━━━━━━
EXAMINER: What do you usually do in the evenings?
SAMARA: Well, I usually do my homework first, and then I chat with my friends online.
EXAMINER: Do you enjoy travelling?
SAMARA: Well, I love seeing new places, but I really don't like long flights.
EXAMINER: What's your favourite subject?
SAMARA: Well, it has to be history, because our teacher makes every lesson feel like a story.
EXAMINER: Do you get a lot of homework?
SAMARA: To be honest, yes, far too much! Most evenings it takes me about two hours.
EXAMINER: Do you ever watch sport on TV?
SAMARA: To be honest, not really. I'd much rather play sport than watch it.
EXAMINER: Are you a morning person?
SAMARA: To be honest, no. I need at least two alarms to get out of bed.

━━━━━━━━━━ BATCH 2 · Let me think / It depends, really ━━━━━━━━━━
EXAMINER: Have you seen a good film recently?
SAMARA: Let me think... Yes! Last weekend I saw a comedy with my cousins, and we couldn't stop laughing.
EXAMINER: Is there anything you'd like to study in the future?
SAMARA: Let me think... Maybe medicine, because I'd like a job where I can help people.
EXAMINER: What's the most interesting place you've travelled to?
SAMARA: Let me think... Probably Rome. There's history on every corner.
EXAMINER: Do you prefer going by car or by public transport?
SAMARA: It depends, really. If I'm in a hurry, the car, but the bus is better for the planet.
EXAMINER: Would you rather work for a big or a small company?
SAMARA: It depends, really. A big company might pay more, but in a small one you'd know everyone.
EXAMINER: Do you prefer individual or team sports?
SAMARA: It depends, really. I like the team spirit, but running on my own helps me relax.

━━━━━━━━━━ BATCH 3 · I'd say / Something like ━━━━━━━━━━
EXAMINER: Who are you most like in your family?
SAMARA: I'd say my dad. We're both quite calm, and we both love bad jokes.
EXAMINER: What's the most popular sport in your country?
SAMARA: I'd say football, without a doubt. Everyone watches the big matches.
EXAMINER: How much time do you spend listening to music?
SAMARA: I'd say about two hours a day, mostly on the way to school.
EXAMINER: What's your home like?
SAMARA: It's quite small, but we have something like a tiny garden on the roof.
EXAMINER: Is there a sport or hobby you enjoy?
SAMARA: Yes, I do something like yoga, but it's faster and you jump a lot.
EXAMINER: Is there anything you'd change about your city?
SAMARA: I'd add something like a big park in the centre, where people could just relax.
