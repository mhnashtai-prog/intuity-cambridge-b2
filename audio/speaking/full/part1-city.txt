B2 FIRST SPEAKING · PART 1 · FULL INTERVIEW (City)
Examiner: Daniel · Candidate A: female voice · Candidate B: male voice

EXAMINER: What's your city like?

CANDIDATE B: It's quite quiet, because I live in Machico, not Funchal. It's right next to the airport, but after a while you stop noticing the planes. When it's windy and the planes can't land, half the town goes out to watch!

EXAMINER: Where's the best place to go with friends in your city?

CANDIDATE A: Definitely the Lido. It's a big outdoor pool right next to the sea. It's cheap, it's close to the centre, and there's always someone from school there. Last summer we went almost every day, and we stayed until they closed the gates.

EXAMINER: Do you travel far every day?

CANDIDATE B: Yes, quite far. I live in Camacha, but my school is in Funchal. The bus takes about forty minutes, but I don't mind, because my school has a great music programme. Last week I fell asleep on the bus and missed my stop!

EXAMINER: Is there anything you'd change about your city?

CANDIDATE A: Yes, I would definitely improve the buses. They're often late, and some hills are so steep that it's difficult to walk. Last week I waited forty minutes in São Martinho and missed the start of my Maths test.

EXAMINER: Do you prefer going by car or by public transport?

CANDIDATE B: The car, definitely. My dad drives me to football training twice a week, and to matches at the weekend. With my sports bag and my boots, the bus would be very difficult, and it takes twice as long.

EXAMINER: How much time do you spend listening to music?

CANDIDATE A: Quite a lot, probably about two hours a day. It helps me concentrate when I'm doing homework, and it makes the bus journey faster. But my taste changes all the time. At the moment I mostly listen to Portuguese pop.
