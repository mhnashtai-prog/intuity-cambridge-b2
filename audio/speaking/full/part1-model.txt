B2 FIRST SPEAKING · PART 1 · THE MODEL INTERVIEW (Funchal)
Beatriz: the safe answer · Tomás: the stronger answer

EXAMINER: What's your city like?
TOMÁS: My city? Oh, I love it! I find Funchal very beautiful. What I like most about it is that it's right next to the sea. Especially in the evenings, when my friends and I walk along the seafront. Although it has become more crowded with tourists, it still feels like home.

EXAMINER: Where's the best place to go with friends in your city?
BEATRIZ: Oh, I love the Lido. The thing I love about it is the swimming pool. Especially in summer, when it's very hot.

EXAMINER: Do you travel far every day?
TOMÁS: Far? Oh yes, quite far, unfortunately! The thing is, I live up in Monte, but my school is in the centre. For example, yesterday the bus took forty minutes! However, I've become more used to it, and now I just listen to music on the way.

EXAMINER: Is there anything you'd change about your city?
BEATRIZ: Hmm, let me think… I'd like better buses. The thing is, they're often late. Mainly because there aren't enough drivers.

EXAMINER: Do you prefer going by car or by public transport?
TOMÁS: To be honest, I'm not a big fan of buses, so I prefer the car. The thing I love about it is that it's much faster. Especially on Saturdays, when my dad drives me to football training. The only problem is that it's worse for the environment.

EXAMINER: How much time do you spend listening to music?
BEATRIZ: Oh, I love music! I listen to it every day, for about two hours. Mainly because it helps me relax.
