A B2 First article is written for a magazine or a website, between one hundred and forty and one hundred and ninety words. Its main aim is to interest the reader, so it has a catchy title, speaks directly to the reader, and sounds lively and personal. In these examples, the article is about the best way to spend a free weekend.

The introduction hooks the reader, often with a question. Here is an example.
<break time="1.0s" />
Who has never imagined a weekend with no homework and no alarm clock? There seems to be something irresistible about two whole days of freedom.
<break time="1.0s" />
The body makes it personal, with your own feelings and experiences. Here is an example.
<break time="1.0s" />
One of the key things I love about a free weekend is exploring somewhere new. I'll never forget the time when my friends and I found a hidden waterfall just an hour from home.
<break time="1.0s" />
The conclusion speaks to the reader again and leaves a lasting impression. For a more extended version, two expressions can be combined into one sentence.
<break time="1.0s" />
In short, there's something truly unforgettable about a weekend outdoors, so if you haven't tried it, make sure you do. You won't regret it!

When you are ready, you can try these expressions on the cards.
