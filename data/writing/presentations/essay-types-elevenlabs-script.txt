In a problems and solutions essay, the body suggests solutions and explains what they would achieve. In these examples, the question is about the problems that tourism causes. Each paragraph opens with a solution. The next sentence shows the result. Here are three examples.
<break time="1.0s" />
One possible solution to this problem would be to limit the number of visitors at popular sites. If such measures were implemented, overcrowding could be significantly reduced.
<break time="1.0s" />
The government should take steps to protect beaches and forests. These actions would help minimise the negative effects of tourism.
<break time="1.0s" />
For a more extended version, the solution and its result can share one sentence. Although public awareness campaigns may not solve the problem completely, they would certainly help tourists to respect local communities.

In a cause and effect essay, the body explains why something happens and what it leads to. In these examples, the question is why tourism has grown so quickly. Each paragraph opens with a cause. A linker then shows the effect. Here are three examples.
<break time="1.0s" />
One of the main causes of the growth in tourism is cheap air travel. As a result, short trips abroad have become increasingly common.
<break time="1.0s" />
Another important cause of this trend concerns social media. Consequently, this has led to huge crowds at the most photographed places.
<break time="1.0s" />
For a more extended version, the cause and the effect can share one sentence. This growth largely results from cheaper travel, which in turn causes higher prices for local residents.

In an opinion essay, every paragraph supports your own view. In these examples, the question asks whether tourism is good for young people. Each paragraph opens with an expression that states your opinion firmly. A reason or an example then backs it up. Here are three examples.
<break time="1.0s" />
I firmly believe that tourism plays an essential role in the modern world. This is because it helps people understand other cultures.
<break time="1.0s" />
From my perspective, travel should be considered a vital part of education. For example, a school trip abroad can teach more than a textbook.
<break time="1.0s" />
For a more extended version, the opinion and the reason can share one sentence. There is no doubt in my mind that tourism is beneficial for young people, since it makes them more independent and open-minded.
