A B2 First review is a short text, between one hundred and forty and one hundred and ninety words, written for a magazine, a newsletter or a website. It describes an experience, shares a personal opinion, and ends with a clear recommendation.

A review usually begins by speaking to the reader. The first sentence tries to engage them and names what is being reviewed. The second sentence introduces it, often by saying what it is known for. Here are three examples.
<break time="1.0s" />
Choosing a café can be a real challenge; however, Café Azul is certainly an option worth considering. Known for its homemade cakes, it has been a favourite among teenagers for years.
<break time="1.0s" />
When it comes to cafés, few options can compete with Café Azul. Located in the old town, it remains an emblematic meeting place for locals and tourists.
<break time="1.0s" />
For a more extended version, the two parts can be combined into one sentence. If you are looking for a relaxing café, then Café Azul, regarded as one of the best, could be the perfect option for you.
<break time="1.5s" />
The body of a review usually describes two features, one in each paragraph. Each paragraph opens with an expression that names the feature. A linker then carries the idea on naturally, with a reason, an example, or a small drawback. Here are three examples.
<break time="1.0s" />
What impressed me most was the food, which is absolutely mouth-watering. This is because everything is made fresh every morning.
<break time="1.0s" />
If you happen to appreciate good coffee, their espresso is by far the most delicious I have tasted. For instance, even a simple cup feels like a treat there.
<break time="1.0s" />
For a more extended version, the feature and the drawback can share one sentence. By far the most appealing aspect of Café Azul is its sunny terrace overlooking the harbour, although it can get quite crowded at weekends.
<break time="1.5s" />
The final paragraph answers the question the reader has been asking: should I try it? It recommends it, says who will enjoy it most, and does not introduce anything new. Here are three examples.
<break time="1.0s" />
This is a café which you cannot afford to miss for its homemade cakes. It is ideal for students looking for a quiet place to relax.
<break time="1.0s" />
For anyone who is interested in good coffee, Café Azul is a must. It is perfect for a relaxed afternoon with friends.
<break time="1.0s" />
For a more extended version, the recommendation can sum up the whole review. I can assure you that Café Azul, with its delicious cakes and sunny terrace, will leave a lasting impression on you, whether you are a local or just visiting.

When you are ready, you can try these expressions on the cards.
