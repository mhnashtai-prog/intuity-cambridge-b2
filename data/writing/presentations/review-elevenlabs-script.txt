A review answers one question for the reader: should I try it, or not?
<break time="0.8s" />
In Part 2 you might review a film, a book, a restaurant, an app or a place. You write for the readers of a magazine or website, people like you. So be lively, personal and honest.
<break time="0.8s" />
Every review does three things. Introduce it. Describe what stands out. And recommend it, or warn people off.
<break time="0.8s" />
One hundred and forty to one hundred and ninety words. Four paragraphs.
<break time="1.5s" />
Your first paragraph has two jobs. First, catch the reader. Pick a line from column A. Choosing a restaurant can be a real challenge. However, Café Azul is certainly an option worth considering.
<break time="0.8s" />
Second, introduce it. Say what it is and why people know it. Known for its homemade cakes, it has been a favourite among teenagers for years.
<break time="0.8s" />
Name the thing in the first two lines, and keep writing about it all the way to the end. A reader who doesn't know what you're reviewing stops reading.
<break time="1.5s" />
Now the body. Two paragraphs, one feature each.
<break time="0.8s" />
Step one, name the feature. Step two, describe it. What impressed me most was the food, which is absolutely mouth-watering. This is where your adjectives live.
<break time="0.8s" />
Then choose one or two of these. A reason: this is because everything is made fresh. An example: for instance, the banana cake is still warm. Or a contrast: the only drawback is that it gets crowded.
<break time="0.8s" />
A fair review gives one negative too. It makes your praise believable. Tap Paragraph structure to see all six steps.
<break time="1.5s" />
The last paragraph answers the reader's question. Should I go?
<break time="0.8s" />
Pick a line from Recommendation. For anyone who is interested in good coffee, Café Azul is a must. Say who will love it, and why, in one short sentence.
<break time="0.8s" />
Never add a new feature here. And never end with: that's all, thank you for reading.
<break time="0.8s" />
