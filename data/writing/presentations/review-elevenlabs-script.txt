A review answers one question for the reader: should I try it, or not? A film, a book, a restaurant, an app, a place. It has four paragraphs. Introduce it. Describe one feature, then another. And recommend it, or warn people off. One hundred and forty to one hundred and ninety words.
<break time="1.5s" />
Paragraph one. Column A: catch the reader. Roll through the openers, and pick one. Choosing a restaurant can be a real challenge. However, Café Azul is certainly an option worth considering.
<break time="1.0s" />
Then column B: introduce it. Known for its homemade cakes, it has been a favourite among teenagers. Name it in the first two lines, and keep writing about it to the end.
<break time="1.5s" />
Paragraphs two and three: one feature each. Name the feature, then describe it. What impressed me most was the food, which is absolutely mouth-watering.
<break time="1.0s" />
Then add one or two of these. A reason: this is because everything is made fresh. An example: for instance, the banana cake is still warm. And one negative: the only drawback is that it gets crowded. It makes your praise believable.
<break time="1.5s" />
Last paragraph: answer the reader's question. Should I go? Roll the recommendations and pick one. For anyone who is interested in good coffee, Café Azul is a must.
<break time="1.0s" />
Never add a new feature here. And never end with: that's all, thank you for reading.
<break time="1.0s" />
Now open the cards. Roll, pick, and fill.
