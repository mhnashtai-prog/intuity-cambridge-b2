A B2 First story is written for a magazine or a website, between one hundred and forty and one hundred and ninety words. You are usually given the first sentence, and your story must begin with it. In these examples, it is: When he saw the postman coming up to his door, Matt ran to open it.

The first paragraph sets the scene, and it uses three past tenses. The past simple tells the events. The past continuous paints the background. The past perfect goes back to what had happened before. Here is an example.
<break time="1.0s" />
When he saw the postman coming up to his door, Matt ran to open it. He had been waiting for the letter for weeks. Outside, rain was falling softly and the whole street was still sleeping.
<break time="1.5s" />
The middle of the story builds the tension. It often uses a suspense word such as suddenly, an expression of time, a verb of movement with an adverb, and a feeling. Here is an example.
<break time="1.0s" />
While the postman was searching his bag, Matt waited nervously on the step. Suddenly, a gust of wind blew the letters across the street. A few seconds later, Matt dashed desperately after them. To his surprise, one of them had his name on it.
<break time="1.5s" />
The ending tells the reader how it all ended, and how the character felt. It is best to avoid an ending like: and then I woke up. Here is an example.
<break time="1.0s" />
To his delight, the letter was from his best friend in Canada. In the end, they spoke for hours that evening. It was a day he would never forget.

When you are ready, you can try these expressions on the cards.
