How it works
  1. Each slot already searches with a query that matches its brief. Change the words or switch the library if nothing fits.
  2. Pick the photo that matches the brief best — the answers describe these people, so a girl should be a girl, two hikers should be two.
  3. At the bottom, choose your part2-turn.json and press Add photos to my file. You get the file back with each photo, its author and its licence written in. Upload it to skills/speaking/data/.
If a photo is close but not exact (three hikers instead of two, say), pick it anyway and tell me — I'll adjust the answer to match.
