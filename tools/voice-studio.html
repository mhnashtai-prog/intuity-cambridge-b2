Voice Studio

Turns the Speaking recording list into correctly named MP3s with your ElevenLabs voices, and gives you one zip to send back. Nothing here is seen by students.

1Your ElevenLabs key

Find it in ElevenLabs under your profile → API keys. It stays in this browser tab and is sent only to ElevenLabs.

2Choose a voice for each speaker

From your ElevenLabs voices. ▶ plays the voice's own sample. Pick distinct voices so students can tell the speakers apart.

3Choose what to record

Start small, for example the examiner lines, and check how they sound in the app before recording everything. Lines already in the app are skipped.
